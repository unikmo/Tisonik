How this is worked out
  • Figures are per voyage unless marked per year. Annual figures multiply by voyages per ship and number of ships.
  • Printing saved is your all-in printing cost per voyage multiplied by the share the card replaces. That covers your cruise app as well as the card.
  • The results lead with the ship's own benefits. Card sales are optional and are shown on top, never mixed into the first two results.
  • Card commission uses fixed defaults for the share of guests who buy, the basket and the discount, and is shown on top. Taxes are excluded.
  • Paper avoided is net of the card and envelope UNIKMO adds. Carbon uses our default factors, so treat it as a rough guide, not a certified figure.
  • Rows marked as estimates depend on your inputs. UNIKMO has not verified any default shown here, and a pilot is how these get measured.

What Tisonik is

      
Not another app. One add-on inside the app guests already use.

      
Tisonik is a white-label guest-experience interaction layer that integrates with the cruise line’s existing app. There is no separate guest download and no replacement of the operator’s booking or commerce stack. The cruise line retains control of its brand, app, inventory, pricing, checkout and payment. A card on each bed is how guests find it: they scan it, enter the welcome code for their trip and see a message from your cruise director, and the same card carries the voyage keepsake home.

FAQs

      
Questions, answered briefly.

    

    

      
What is the card?
A printed paper card, one on each bed. A QR code on the back opens a message from the cruise director. A welcome code, created for each trip and written on the card, ties it to the voyage, and it works only for cruise cards. On departure guests take the keepsake home with them.

      
Do guests need to download anything?
No. The card opens the message, and Tisonik runs inside the cruise line’s existing app.

      
Who stores the messages and keepsake, and who owns them?
UNIKMO stores them under its own process and keeps media for as long as possible, with no fixed limit. The cruise line owns them.

      
Who prints the cards and gets them to the ship?
UNIKMO prints the cards and delivers them to the ship, with a lead time of two weeks at most. Ship staff place one on each bed.

      
What does the card cost?
5.00 per guest per voyage.

      
Where does Tisonik run?
The passenger experience runs inside the cruise line’s existing app as a white-label SDK or embedded module. The card is a printed paper card that opens the cruise director’s message and the keepsake; the feature itself still runs inside the cruise line’s app. Identity resolution and source actions run on-device, ship-local or in the operator’s systems. Tisonik cloud receives only aggregate KPI reports and non-identifying service-health telemetry.
